Privacy Policy

Creator Station is a personal tool used only by the owner of the JailBall YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It only requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly, to upload the channel's own videos, read the channel name and upload status, and read the channel's own analytics (views, retention). No data is collected, shared or sold; the access token stays on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok: with the owner's permission (TikTok login), the scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list and video.upload, to read the account's name and statistics, list its own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, and send its own videos as drafts. Tokens stay on the owner's computer. Revoke at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services.

Creator Station's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
